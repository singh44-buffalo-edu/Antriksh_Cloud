What We Do

Built for India.
Built for AI.

India has the world's highest AI adoption rate — yet 90% of its AI runs on foreign servers, costing Indian companies 3–4× more, adding over 200ms of latency to every request, and sending sensitive data overseas. Antriksh Cloud is India's first liquid-cooled, sovereign GPU infrastructure: H100, H200, and GB-series clusters in Delhi NCR, priced 40–50% below hyperscalers, with your data guaranteed to never leave India.

We are not a general-purpose cloud. Every rack, every networking decision, every cooling system was designed for one thing — running AI workloads at the density, speed, and compliance standard that India's builders actually need. Whether you are a startup training your first model or an enterprise deploying AI at scale, Antriksh gives you the infrastructure to build without compromise.
